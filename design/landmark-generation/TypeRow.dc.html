A DIMMED ROW WITH A DASH IS NOT A BUG

This is the same treatment menus.gd:233's _todo already gives an unbacked menu row — “Disabled, with the reason attached, so the menu never promises behaviour that does not exist.”

THE CAP LADDER
off · 1 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 8 · 12 · 20 · 30 · 50 · 80 · 120 · 200
A linear 0–200 track spends most of its travel where nobody wants to be. On a 1–2–3–5 ladder the step from 1 to 2 gets the same travel as the step from 120 to 200 — which is right, because for a CON-class type one versus two is the design of the world and 120 versus 200 means nothing. The exact integer stays typeable in the Cap field at the top of the fold, so no value is unreachable.
