ONE DRAFT, TWO SURFACES — THE RULE
Commit and Discard appear twice, and that is deliberate: the bar is where the hand is and the dock is where the count is. The rule that makes it safe is that every surface over one draft refreshes together — a commit from the bar left the right dock reporting “1 dab pending” with a live Commit over an emptied draft, and rebuilt that dock zero times.
Never a third copy in the left dock. That is what the removed panel was.
