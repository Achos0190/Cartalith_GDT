WHAT IS REAL, AND WHERE IT COMES FROM. The marks are get_civ_timeline_years(), ascending, one per recorded snapshot. The counts on a
recorded year are a single civ_year_diff(y) call — present, added, removed, diffed against the
chronologically previous recorded year. The cursor is get_civ_year() / civ_goto_year(); the track is
−400…1200, fixed and continuous, so the cursor lands anywhere and the territory
under it changes only on a recorded year.

WHAT IS NOT, AND IS THEREFORE NOT DRAWN. A named event. A snapshot carries no title, no
category and no author — nothing in cartalith-civ holds one — so no mark here is
labelled and none is coloured by kind. Per-mark magnitude. That needs one
civ_year_diff per mark on every repaint, and the repaint runs per motion event
mid-drag; the magnitude is stated once instead, for the year under the cursor.
A removed settlement’s name or place. civ_year_diff returns tid sets only.

MARK COLLISION. civ_run_collapse_simulation writes one entry per step, so a run
can leave marks 5 years apart — about 2 px on a 700 px track. Colliding marks merge
and are never fattened: a fatter mark would read as a bigger year.
