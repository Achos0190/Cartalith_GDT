Arming pan also raises the existing phone toast (app.gd · _show_phone_toast, 4.5 s): one finger pans — tap again to give it back to the tool. A latched mode with no announcement is the failure a toggle-only repaint already is.
FOUR CONTROLS, FOUR JUSTIFICATIONS
−  ZOOM OUT stepped and repeatable where pinch is continuous; one-handed, where a pinch is not. Already bound — zoom_step(1 / ZOOM_BUTTON_STEP)
＋  ZOOM IN the same, and the reachable half of the pair — a rocker keeps them adjacent, so a repeated tap needs no re-aim
✋  PAN LATCH not a duplicate of drag. Two fingers pan; one finger is given to the armed tool on purpose. This latch is the only one-finger pan the handset has — which is the one-handed case exactly
⌖  RECENTRE no gesture recovers a lost view; a pinch cannot undo a pan. reset_view() exists, and clears pan mode with it
