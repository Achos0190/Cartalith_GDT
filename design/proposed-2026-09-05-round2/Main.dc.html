THE CASE FOR A. One dock, one rule. Nothing WORLD owns can
ever be in “the other mode”, so a control you cannot find is a control you have not
scrolled to — a state the scrollbar already reports. One grammar across three
domains. CIVIL’s eleven categories and CARTO’s four are accordion-only; A makes
WORLD the same, and a user who learns one dock has learned all three. Fewer
states to keep true. The mode gate, apply_mode, the pill’s three entry routes and
the probe that asserts no category is stranded all collapse into nothing.
WHAT A COSTS, HONESTLY. The column is long, and sculpting is at
the bottom of it. Six sculpt sections sit under a category that is itself under
eight collapsed headers. Arming Sculpt has to scroll the dock rather than
switch it, and a column that moves under you is a weaker signal than a body
that changes. Erosion dials and sculpt tools share one column. The dials
mark the world stale on the next Generate; the tools do not. A draws no line
between them. And it drops the spec: SS2.1 band 2, SS2.3 and SS3 rows 1–2 all
describe the switch, and A silently declines all three.
