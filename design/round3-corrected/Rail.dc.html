WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO, AND WHY
It does not replace the nodes with domain labels. That was the earlier proposal, taken from the register and reasoned from “there is nothing to build”. There is: ten nodes, each with a mode and an owned category list, shipped and reachable. Replacing them would trade navigation for description.
It does not restyle the labels. The earlier canvas moved rail text to the theme's sans; the shipped rows already follow 02-rail-and-domains.md §5d at the point they are built. Headers stay tracked mono, nodes stay sans. No change is proposed, so no owner call is needed.
No phone surface. The bottom bar names PHONE_TABS, deliberately not DOMAINS, and the app bar's one subtitle slot already carries the world name and seed. Explaining a domain the user just tapped is not worth the seed line. Absent by decision, as before.
