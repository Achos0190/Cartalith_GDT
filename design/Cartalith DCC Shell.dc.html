TOP 44 PX · KEEP CLEAR

SCRIM, NOT A BAR
A top gradient carries legibility over imagery, so the map is not cropped by an opaque strip.
BOTTOM 26 PX · GESTURE INSET
No tappable target within the gesture area. Sheets and the timeline stop above it.
LANDSCAPE
The cutout moves to a side edge: apply the same reserve horizontally — the domain rail shifts inward and the map keeps bleeding under it.
TARGETS
44 px minimum, measured inside the safe area. Slider thumbs grow to 19 px; the sheet is the only place controls stack.
