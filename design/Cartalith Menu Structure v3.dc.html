WORLD — WHAT EXISTS
The physical and environmental state. Terrain, geology, water, climate, biomes, resources, and the generation that produces them. Authoritative for every measured field.
CIVIL — WHO OCCUPIES IT
Factions, territories, settlements and POIs — and the ways, routes, trade and travel that connect them. Movement is a civilizational fact, so logistics is a CIVIL concern, not a fourth domain.
CARTO — HOW IT IS SHOWN
Presentation over both datasets. Colour, relief, labels, layer stack, asset rendering, zoom rules. Holds no world truth and marks no generation stage stale.
DEPENDENCY DIRECTION
WORLD → CIVIL, and both → CARTO.
CARTO reads WORLD and CIVIL. Neither reads CARTO. No dataset is copied for rendering — a style change writes only to the style document. Narrative text lives in the vault, referenced by path.

MIGRATION AUDIT · V2.10 SURFACE → V3 OWNER10 blocks reassigned
V2.10 SURFACENOW OWNED BYWHAT CHANGED, AND WHY
PROJECTprogram scopeFile, import, export, undo and theme were never world state. They return to the menu bar and stop competing with domains for rail space.
WORLD (planet, calibration)WORLD ▸ World DataKept whole. Generation controls that sat here move up into WORLD ▸ Generate so seed, steering and bake read as one act.
GENERATE · geology & waterWORLDSplit by subject rather than by run order: tectonics and volcanism to Geology, all erosion passes to Terrain, rivers and coast to Hydrology. The numbered 01–10 stage list disappears as navigation and survives as pipeline status.
GENERATE · climate & ecologyWORLDClimate, weather, ecology and biome classification stay physical. Only the biome colours leave, for CARTO.
CIVILIZATION · sub-tabs
#civSubBar — Factions · Generation · Settlements · Economy · StatisticsCIVILThe five tabs become named categories instead of a flat tab bar. Generation Step 1 becomes Civilizations, Step 2 becomes Routes & Ways, Step 3 becomes Territories. Its Display accordion — icon scale, way line width, per-layer opacity — is the one part that leaves, for CARTO. split
Ways & routes
#civWayList · #civWayType · #civCommitWayBtn · #civCommitRouteBtnCIVIL ▸ Routes & WaysStays exactly where v2.10 put it. Roads are built by people, so the network is civilizational data — there is no separate logistics domain to move it to.
EXPLORE · journeys, planner, timelineCIVIL + programsplit. Journeys, the planner and the travel library become CIVIL ▸ Travel. Collapse/recovery becomes CIVIL ▸ Simulation. The year scrubber and existence filter are program scope — every domain reads the year.
MAP · cartographyCARTOBecomes the spine of CARTO. Its scattered show-flags collapse into the layer stack; its label list gains per-category rules; its manual icons become CIVIL POIs drawn with CARTO symbols.
MAP · terrain appearanceCARTOKept whole, unchanged in scope. It was already a correct presentation-only surface and sets the pattern for the rest of CARTO.
ASSETSprogram + CARTOsplit. The library — import, slice, tag, validate, export — is a program window. Which asset draws where, at what scale and density, is CARTO ▸ Assets & Landmarks.
VIEW3 ownerssplit. Viewport dimension, LOD toggles and performance readouts are program scope. Tile refine and atlas bake produce terrain data, so they belong to WORLD ▸ Generate. Analysis overlays are presentation and move to CARTO ▸ Visibility.
CONTEXT-SENSITIVE EDITING
MOUNTAIN · SLOPEWORLD ▸ Terrain readout at the point, with Geology beneath it.
RIVER · LAKEWORLD ▸ Hydrology: discharge, catchment, tributaries, navigability.
SETTLEMENTCIVIL settlement editor — Site band reading WORLD live, vault note previewed below it.
TERRITORYCIVIL political editor, with a link to its CARTO display rule.
WAYCIVIL way record — endpoints, class, upkeep, crossings — plus the CARTO style row for its class.
ROUTECIVIL ▸ Travel: the journey planner opens on that route.
LABELCARTO label editor. The name itself opens its owning object.
LANDMARK ASSETCARTO asset controls, with the POI or settlement it stands for one click away.
Selection never forces a rail change. The inspector switches domain; the viewport and the rail stay where the user left them.
OWNERSHIP RULES
ONE WRITEREach field has exactly one owner that may write it. Everyone else shows it read-only, with the owner one click away.
NO COPIESCARTO stores style documents keyed to object ids, never a second copy of elevation, biome or population.
STALE FLAGSOnly WORLD and CIVIL edits mark stages stale. No CARTO control may invalidate a generation stage.
SITE IS READA settlement's elevation, biome, water and resources are never typed in. They are sampled, and change when the world changes.
VAULT IS PROSEIf the model never reads it, it belongs in the note, not in a form field. The app stores the path and writes frontmatter; it never rewrites the body.
MODEL VS DATAA simulation lives with its inputs; its results live with the entity. CIVIL ▸ Simulation runs collapse, the settlement holds the population it produced.
SAME OBJECTSwitching rail keeps the selection. WORLD, CIVIL and CARTO are three inspectors over one dataset, not three applications.
Disclosure grammar unchanged from v2: L2 ▾ category, L3 § section, L4 › group, L5 + advanced, five levels maximum. Every #id keeps its wiring — this is re-parenting, not rewriting.
