+/// **Not the reference** (owner Ruling HB Q23 (a), 2026-11-22): a water gate
+/// at every point where a live water lane ([`lay_back_street_with`] with
+/// `water_gate`) crosses the finished circuit `ring`, so
+/// `cleanup::clear_fort_zone` keeps the lane (it kills an edge crossing the
+/// ring with no gate within its reach). Called after the wall is refitted and
+/// set on the shore, so the gates stand on the circuit that is drawn. Gates
+/// are `water: true`: the reference draws no water gate, and the wall lots
+/// (`crate::wallside`) read only the land gates. Must never open a gate on an
+/// edge that is not a water lane.
+pub fn water_gates(g: &Graph, ring: &[Vec2]) -> Vec<Gate> {
+    g.edges

+        .collect()
