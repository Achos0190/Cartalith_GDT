mean HSV saturation; grad = mean neighbour luma gradient; hf2 = sd of luma
minus a 5x5 box blur; lc8 = sd of luma minus a 17x17 box blur (local/relief
contrast); classdist = mean RGB distance of the five class means below to the
target's. Medians over frames.
