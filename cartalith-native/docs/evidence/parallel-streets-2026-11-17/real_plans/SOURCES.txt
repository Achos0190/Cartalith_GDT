Scale of the Bavarian sheets: town sheets (Ortsblaetter) were drawn at 1:2 500 and the field sheets at 1:5 000
covering 800 Ruten = 2 334.87 m a side (de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayerische_Uraufnahme, read 2026-11-17). A 1:2 500 sheet
of the same paper size covers about 1 167 m, so about 0.47 m per pixel of a 2 500 px scan if the scan is the whole
sheet [inference: the scans include margins, so treat any metre figure taken from them as +/- 15 %]. Some Franconian
town sheets were drawn at 1:1 250 (same page); Dinkelsbuehl's church (St. Georg) at about 140 px rules that out
for its sheet [inference].
