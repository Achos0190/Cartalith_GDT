Ruling FN (owner, 2026-11-16): river towns may stretch along their river beyond the square town box where terrain
allows, so the town judgement's 15 % riverside bar becomes reachable. Built as a PROTOTYPE behind a new switch,
urban_river_stretch, OFF in WorldParams::defaults AND in params::defaults (the app): it misses the 15 % bar and
three of the must-hold bars, so it waits on the owner. Measured 2026-11-16.

Census: cartalith-godot town_river_census (both_banks_audit, town_housing_audit, riverbank_gap_audit,
harbour_front_audit), seeds 483920, 912345, 246371, 800 km, villages on (TOWN_RIVER_VILLAGES=1), release test
binary, the app's other switches (params::defaults), at 384 x 288 and 2048 x 1311 (audit.sh.txt). Before = the
same binary with TOWN_RIVER_STRETCH=0, which is the reference box by control flow (its numbers equal the town
leftovers' "after" at origin/main 12cb6983: 2.5 % / 2.5 %, wall / hull p90 1.12 / 1.16). After = TOWN_RIVER_STRETCH=1.
Full outputs: before/ and after/.

What the prototype does (cartalith_urban::stretch, cartalith_civ::urban_adapter::river_stretch_plan):
 1. A river town (site kind river or riverthrough, a real centreline, urban_riverbank on, no sea in its reference
    box) of STRETCH_MIN_POP (1 500) and up is planned on a probe box: target depth across the river
    STRETCH_ACROSS_M (450 m), reach along it max_rf^2 / 450 (the town's area kept), capped at
    STRETCH_ALONG_MAX_RF (1.5) x max_rf each way; each side walked along the centreline every 20 m and cut where
    the river enters open water, both banks are unbuildable for more than STRETCH_GAP_M (120 m), or another
    settlement's reach (+120 m) or the half-way line to it is reached. The depth widens back to keep the area
    when a side is cut; a gain under 1.15 x max_rf drops the stretch. With 450 m only towns of about 5 000 and up
    actually stretch (max_rf over 517 m).
 2. The town is laid out in a box holding the stretched region plus 150 m (centred on the market; the City
    Viewer frame and the map's town layer read the layout's own wm / hm and pin), from that box's water and
    relief; the host's roads are followed to its edge.
 3. Every stage that zones the town by its distance from the market reads a river-following distance
    (Site::market_dist: max_rf * hypot(along / reach, across / depth)): the growth front and density gradient,
    the far bank's growth, the districts' rings, the interior-lane and wall-mass cut-offs, the plot depth's core,
    the courtyard ring and the hinterland's urban test.
 4. A road along the market's bank, 40 m back from the channel's edge (the waterfront back street's band), is
    laid before the town grows (stretch::lay_river_spine): without it the growth tree never reached along the
    river (fixture stretch::tests at a 300 m / 1 300 m plan: 6 776 people against the unstretched 10 795).

Arithmetic (why 15 % is out of reach for a town that does not look like a noodle): a fully lined river front is
about (bank row depth) / (town depth across the river) of a town's houses, whatever its size. The plain town is a
half disc about max_rf (720 m) deep: 2.5 %. At 450 m target depth (one bank about 300-450 m deep) the census
reached 5.1 %. 15 % needs a town about 140-200 m deep on each bank, 3-4 km long for 20 000 people: the variant below.

The steeper variant (variant_2x_300m/: STRETCH_ACROSS_M 300, STRETCH_ALONG_MAX_RF 2.0, at 384 only):
6.7 % riverside; housed p10 74, under 80 % 6, over 200 % 1, p90 188; wall / hull p90 1.15, max 1.34. Its plans
(pair_*.png, off | on, census world, same scale) are walled cores with long ragged ribbon suburbs of dead-end
lanes along the river road, 246371 #11 housing 12 522 of its 18 568 (from 19 287): a noodle and a sprawl.
