What the fix is (cartalith_urban::stretch::stretch_holds, cartalith_civ::urban_adapter::run_layout):
 The prototype's worse rows came from single sites, so the fix tests each finished layout against Ruling FQ's bars:
 housed >= 0.9 x target; walled: wall <= 1.12 x its own convex hull, the square inside the wall (5.4 m), no unbuilt
 patch inside the wall over 0.5 ha (unbuilt_patch_m2, the census's reading). Each bar is met by its bound OR by being
 no worse than the same town laid out unstretched. Order: the full stretch against the bounds; a milder stretch
 (StretchPlan::milder: half the extra reach, depth widened to keep the area) against the bounds; then the town
 unstretched (UrbanContext::plain, the reference-box context) and each stretch against it; else the unstretched
 layout. Each bound is at or inside the pre-stretch p10 / p90, so no census order statistic can get worse.
 Map: WorldGen.urban_layout_boxes (um_layout_box, the planned box by tid) -> map_overlay.gd: each town claims pins
 within its own reach and is culled by its layout's own box (wm, hm, pin) or, before it has one, its planned box.
