# Grounded place prose: a sanity check of the context and prompt, not a study

**Owner decision, 2026-10-26: the planned live comparison of qwen3-14b and Qwen3.8-27B on this prose was dropped.** The judgement already made from the stage 1 and stage 2 data of the parameter work stands: **Qwen3.8-27B `UD-Q3_K_XL` with thinking off is the local pick, and qwen3-14b with thinking off is the fast fallback.** No ranking is claimed here, and no REPORT.md was written.

This file records only what was run, before the decision arrived, to check that the facts, the Vault notes and the prompt work through the real assistant panel. Everything is single runs on one machine (Ryzen 7 9800X3D, RX 7800 XT 16 GB), LM Studio 0.4.26 with the ROCm 2.64.0 runtime, `lm_studio` preset (`reasoning_effort: "none"` is sent for grounded prose), offload 1.0, context 8192, parallel 1, flash attention on, KV f16, MTP default. Server-default temperature. `_llmplace_live_probe.gd` drove the real panel (a real click on Send), world 2048 x 1311 cells, 800 km, village add-on on, belief model run for 60 years, seeds 71077345 and 24601, four places each (largest, small village, port, highest remote place). Raw records: `raw/`.

## What was checked and what it showed

- **The context reaches the model and both models use it.** Prompt version 1, 24 requests per model (16 without Vault notes, 8 with synthetic notes), none cut at the length limit, none failed. The grounding check matched 598 numbers (counting roundings) and 628 names in the 27B replies and 42 numbers and 270 names in the 14B replies, and found 0 and 1 unverified numbers. Median prompt about 1 930 tokens; the facts are 4 000 to 6 000 characters.
- **Speed through the panel (median of the runs; the second repetition of a request reuses the prompt prefix, so first-token times of 0.05 s are cache hits):** qwen3-14b 16.0 s total, about 44 tok/s; Qwen3.8-27B 25.5 s total, about 38 tok/s (the warm-up check read 50.7 and 38.4 tok/s; the stage 2 tuned reference was 50.6 and 43.7). Replies ran 440 words (14B) and 595 words (27B).
- **One defect, fixed.** Prompt version 1 told the model in every request to end with a `Discrepancy:` sentence where a Vault note and the data disagree. With no notes in the request, **qwen3-14b wrote an invented `Discrepancy:` sentence in 16 of 16 replies and mentioned Vault notes that were never sent in 13 of 16; qwen3.8-27b did neither (0 of 16)**. Version 2 has two system prompts: the Vault clauses exist only when a note is sent, and the no-notes variant forbids mentioning notes. After the change, 8 of 8 no-notes replies from qwen3-14b carried none (`raw/prompt_v2_A_partial.jsonl`, the run was stopped by the owner decision after 12 requests).
- **A planted contradiction** (a note saying the walls are a timber palisade where the data says stone), prompt version 1: qwen3.8-27b named it in 8 of 8 replies with notes; qwen3-14b in 3 of 8 and invented a different discrepancy in the other five. The probe's two place notes shared a folder, so each place's request also received the other's note, which confused the smaller model.
- **Exact figures.** The 27B model stated the place's exact population in 16 of 16 no-notes replies, the 14B model in 9 of 16.
- **Checker false positives found and fixed:** section headings and bold lead-ins (`Daily Life`, `Vault`) were reported as unverified names. The remaining unverified names in the 14B replies are mostly capitalised sentence words (`Though`, `Notes`, `Crossroads`) and invented demonyms (`Korrathi`).
- **Both models invented flavour without marking it** in a few replies (for example a modest fishing village as a place's origin, "likely began as"); the check cannot see an unmarked invention that contains no new name or number.

## Limits

Single runs; two seeds; one machine; ROCm only; a world in which most settlements within 25 km are zero-population hamlets (the reference's own shape); the panel's Save was never clicked in the live runs. The click defect: in the first full run about a third of injected clicks on Send did nothing (button enabled, no message); not reproduced in the later runs, cause not established; the probe records every attempt (`raw/first_run_click_defect/`). Nothing here supports a claim that either model is better than the other beyond the owner's earlier judgement.
