CAUSES, read at the symbols

(c) The near bank shrank. 88fb0ef3 lifted grow's bridgehead rule inside the epoch loop (GrowOpts::far_bank) and laid the town
    bridge before grow. Each epoch's street budget (target_len / epochs) was shared: every far-bank street was a street the
    market's bank did not get, and the bridge's edges changed what grow drew everywhere. The cap (far <= near street length)
    only stopped the far bank outgrowing the near one; it never protected the near one.
(a) The square outside the wall. Three causes. (1) one_bank_by_lots refitted the circuit round the LARGER bank, handing the
    circuit builder a stand-in market there, so a square on the smaller bank was walled out. (2) build_plaza widens the
    nearest primary at its MIDDLE; on a real river the market is pinned at the bridge foot on the bank, so the square stood a
    road's half-length away and sometimes across a confluence from the town (census plots: seed 483920 #0 and #7 at 384).
    (3) The wall hug never counted the square as mass, so a refit fitted to the lots could leave it out. Specialised markets
    (build_markets: junctions 85-300 m from the market) ignored the wall, which once stood well clear of the town; with a
    fitted wall one could straddle the curtain (seed 12345 #0 in the viewer).
(b) The unbuilt patch inside the wall. Two causes. (1) wallhug::envelope is a star about the mass's centre (one radius per
    direction), so a U- or L-shaped town's bend is walled. (2) A circuit across the river is the convex hull of its hull
    (build_wall_from_mass), which walls every bay; and the "take the last widening" fallback of 88fb0ef3 could accept a hull
    whose land arc and bank walk crossed over the town (seed 12345 #0 in the viewer: a ring folded over empty ground).
Villages: trim_overbuilt emptied the outermost lots of over-built villages (about half of them sat at the 125 % cap), which
    draw as a ring of empty plots.

FIXES (cartalith-urban; every one reached only on a riverbank site, so the urban goldens and the FH-R2/G1 harnesses are unmoved)

1. grow is the reference again (GrowOpts::far_bank removed; the street legalization split out as growth::legalize_street
   without moving a draw). generate lays a river town out once; riverbank::second_pass asks for a second layout when a
   both-banks town houses under its pop_target: RiverbankPass::FarBank { share = pop_target/pop - 1, at most 1.0 }, which lays
   the bridge AFTER grow and grows the far bank with growth::grow_far_bank from share x the market bank's street length (own
   random stream, far-bank origins only, buildable ground only, capped at FAR_BANK_MAX_RATIO). The market's bank is grown from
   the same graph and draws as the first layout's.
2. hug_wall on a riverbank site: wallhug::outline (lots filled on a 4 m grid, closed with CLOSE_R_M 40 m, holes filled, eroded
   back to the clearance, opened by 2 cells, traced, simplified) instead of the star; BuiltMass::exact keeps that shape across
   the river; two quarters that face each other across the river are joined over the water they face; untangle cuts the bank
   walk's self-crossing slivers (refuses a loop over 2 % of the area); points within BANK_EXEMPT_M (10 m) of the bank owe no
   clearance; a stray lot or two left in a detached piece (OUTLINE_MINOR_SHARE 5 %) stays outside; the circuit is tried both
   ways (across the river, round the market's bank) before the grown one is kept; the 88fb0ef3 "take the last widening"
   fallback is gone; one_bank_by_lots never walls the other bank alone; a far bank grown after a one-bank circuit is walled
   with the town when it holds enough lots (the same 32 % test).
3. plaza::riverbank_square (chartered towns only): the square on a primary beside the market, on its bank, 15 m back from the
   channel edge, nearest the market. The market square is wall mass, must be in the outline's piece, and a circuit leaving it
   outside is refused. amenities::build_markets_within: specialised markets 30 m inside the wall.
4. riverbank::fit_village: an over-built riverbank village is laid out again from a street budget scaled toward its target
   (secant on the head count, at most 4 layouts, aim 110 %); trim_overbuilt runs only on the layout kept when none lands in
   80-125 %, and that is the over-cap layout housing the fewest (never an under-built one: tried, it took 48 villages from
   125 % to as low as 31 %).

Reading it. The two new bars are met: squares outside the wall 7 -> 4 and 12 -> 2, and the remainder is named above (village
greens are not market squares and are not required inside; the landlocked town is not on this path). Walls round an unbuilt
patch over 1 ha 16 -> 0 and 20 -> 1. Housing: the near bank keeps what the reference grew, so towns are near target at the
median (97 %, 96 %) with a narrower top (p90 131-142 %, was 148-152 %); fewer towns cross (13 and 16 of the big ones): only
those that house under target, as the ruling's "can" allows. Villages: the median falls from the 125 % cap to 105-106 %, none
newly under 80 %, and the empty lots fall by 70 %. Regressions, routed: the setback measures (a house over the widest street
259 -> 298 and 329 -> 355; every house over 2 widths 87 -> 99, 156 -> 168) and the audit's "round an empty bank" (3 -> 4,
5 -> 8) moved the wrong way; not attributed per cause.
