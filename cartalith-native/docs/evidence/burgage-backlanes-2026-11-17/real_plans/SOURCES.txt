Real town plans beside the frames (TOWN_GENERATOR_JUDGEMENT.md section 6: "put two or three real plans beside the
frames"). All three are sheets of the Bavarian cadastral survey (Bayerische Uraufnahme, "Ortsblatt", 1:2 500 town
sheets drawn for the land tax), every plot boundary surveyed, so each burgage plot, back lane and yard can be read.
All public domain (licence read from each file's Wikimedia Commons page through the Commons API on 2026-11-17:
LicenseShortName "Public domain"; the survey office's 1813-1826 work, its authors dead well over 100 years).
Downloaded unchanged (the Commons originals, 2500 px) into this folder only.
