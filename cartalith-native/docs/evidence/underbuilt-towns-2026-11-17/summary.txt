Under-built towns, cause per town (2026-11-17). Investigation only: no product code changed.

WHAT WAS RUN
Temporary diagnostics on a throwaway worktree (diff kept as temp_diagnostics.patch.txt, never committed as code):
refused street origins per epoch and per reason (grow), street length laid vs budget, tries used, the
town-bridge and far-bank pass outcome, route ends, and a dump of the open-water raster. Harness: the
census world (CPU, 800 km, villages on, app defaults), 384x288 and 2048x1311, seeds 483920 912345 246371,
the layout the app lays (urban_riverbank on, stretch on, market_road_bank on). run.sh.txt, an.py.txt,
banks.py.txt, plotref.py.txt reproduce the tables and plans.

WHAT COULD NOT BE DONE
The five APP-WORLD (GPU) towns (912345 #30 at 384; 483920 #8, #0, #11, 246371 #3, 912345 #0 at 2048)
are NOT reproduced: with use_gpu on, the harness builds a world with the same town count (39) but not
the app's towns (no town under 0.78 of the median; #30's target differs), so the app's world has inputs the
harness does not carry. They are classed below by analogy only and need the same diagnostics run from the app
(a probe that prints the final-pass grow table and the open-water raster). The four CENSUS towns reproduce
exactly (246371 #7 at 384; 246371 #0, 483920 #10, 912345 #10 at 2048: census_world_under_median.txt).

CAUSE THAT IS NOT THE CAUSE
- The 2600-tries-per-epoch cap in grow, and 'short<38' picks eating 40-58 % of tries, is real (every town,
  healthy or not, runs out of tries from epoch 3-4 and lays 12-28 km of the 42 km budget; whole_budget is off
  in the app) but it does not separate the under-built from the rest. Ablation: 4x tries moves 246371 #7
  8 970 -> 9 012 housed and 246371 #0 5 782 -> 8 767 (still 44 % of target). Not the cause.
- The stretch frame: STRETCH None on all five. grow's explore minimum (d_m < 60 m): lowering to 0 moves
  246371 #0 5 782 -> 6 536 only.

CAUSE PER CENSUS TOWN (bank_split.txt, grow_diag_per_town.txt, plans/ref_*.png)
All four are targets of 20 000 (max_rf clamps at 720 m, town_reach_m) and all four have a walled one-sided plan.
The town's street front is a disc of 720 m round the market; 20 000 people need about all of it.

 Group A, the front disc is mostly water (market on a coast or lake shore).
  246371 #7 at 384 (coast, 45 %): disc r=720: 53 % open water, 1 % far side, 9 % outside the box margin: 37 % land.
     1 847 buildings for 37 % land, i.e. the land is built about as densely as the healthy towns.
  246371 #0 at 2048 (riverthrough, 29 %): market is 377 m from the river; sea to the south and east: 58 % open water,
     9 % outside the box, 33 % land. Zero street edges south of the market (edges south of y = market+30: 0 of 427);
     both route ends are on the north edge; the town bridge is refused (bridge point is outside the box, 790 m from
     the market, past max_rf 720; BRIDGE none reason 3), so the far (NE) bank gets no far-bank pass.
  483920 #10 at 2048 (riverthrough, 55 %): lake to the west: 28 % open water, 9 % box, far bank 16 % land with 27
     buildings; near bank 47 % land with 2 232 buildings (as dense as a full disc). Far-bank pass ran
     (bridge laid at 446 m south of the market) but laid 390 m for a budget of 0.85 x near bank: the far land
     is a lake-shore strip.
  This is the "market 314-344 m from the river" shape the row names: the market stands on the shore of
  the water body that is NOT the river, so the river tells nothing about the side the town can use.

 Group B, the far bank is land but not grown (market bank full, far bank empty).
  912345 #10 at 2048 (river, 62 %): no open water. Market bank 49 % of the disc, 2 689 buildings (full);
     far bank 42 % of the disc, 55 buildings (2 %). Healthy control 246371 #1: far bank 44 %, 1 049 buildings;
     246371 #2: 43 %, 1 261. The river bends in a V round the market, the bridge point is 50 m from it, and
     lay_town_bridge returns None at its "ends are dry, on opposite banks, 0.5 m past the channel" test
     (BRIDGE none reason 4; which of its three sub-tests fails was not split out), so grow_far_bank never
     runs (no FARBANK line); the reference's bridgehead rule (kind == river: far-bank origins only within
     bridgehead_distance of the bridge point and with bridgehead_probability, 166 refusals) leaves the far
     bank empty.

PROPOSED FIXES (owner questions are in OUTSTANDING_WORK.md)
 A. Scale the front by the land the disc holds: max_rf_eff = max_rf / sqrt(land share of the 720 m disc),
    capped (default x1.6), only where the share is under 0.8. Moves: only water-clipped towns (3 of the 4 under-built census towns; the other
    32 big towns at 2048 and 35 at 384 untouched, byte-identical by control flow. Goes through
    DECISIONS.md section 7p (a re-baseline of those towns' layout pins) and needs an owner ruling.
    Limit shown by the ablation: a town whose box is mostly water (246371 #7) stays near 54 %.
 B. Let the town bridge look for a crossing: when lay_town_bridge's point fails (reason 3 or 4), try the
    nearest centreline vertices within max_rf with both ends valid, instead of giving up. Moves: only
    both-banks towns whose bridge is refused today (about 40 of 60 had none before FB: town-housing evidence).
 C. Accept it: a town whose site cannot hold its target is reported at its real size (a note on the card), and
    its population is unchanged. Moves nothing generated.
 D. (Both groups) A size-aware cap: clamp pop_target for layout to the disc's capacity so the housed share
    reads 100 %. Moves the population shown for those towns only (the settlement's own pop is untouched).
