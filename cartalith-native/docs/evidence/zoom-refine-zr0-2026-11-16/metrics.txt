Frames are 768x768 at 0.471 and 0.078 km/px. native = the native map's own shipped tiles (fg_frame, the shell's level and morph);
fg_noise = the world's shipped FG tiles at the same km/px (level cap 16); zrbase = the ZR chain's steps 2-3 only (upsample + one
FG band per level, no drainage or erosion); zrbw / zref3 = the full chain with the Braun-Willett light solver / EF-3 tile_erode.
The ZR frame is the shell's rule: level round(log2 texels-per-cell) morphed over the level above (0.471: level 5 over 4, morph
0.958; 0.078: level 8 over 7, morph 0.456).

grad   = mean |grad luma| on input-land pixels (forward differences), the FG findings' grad.
ratio_vs_native = grad / the same-terrain native window's grad; ratio_vs_native_whole = grad / the whole native map at fit.
bind_r = ONE definition for every frame (FG's needs an on/off pair; a native map at fit has none, so its B4 bind_r was never
         measured): Pearson r across 32 px land blocks (>= 90 % land) between std(luma - blur16(luma)) and mean |grad blur16(luma)|.
Places, picked by inputs: mountain = highest mean coarse local relief window, plain = lowest, among land windows; pivot = the FG
harness's (0.40, 0.625).
